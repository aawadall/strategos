## Terrain character

Norman bocage (hedgerows), coastal bluffs at Omaha, road net through villages, limited
long-range observation inland, weather/sea state as operational constraints (army-level,
not hex LOS).

## Objectives

- **Allied (army/corps):** Secure lodgement; expand beachhead; set conditions for
  breakout (Operation Cobra is the later army/corps offensive — separate scenario if
  authored).
- **German:** Contain the lodgement; prevent breakout into open country.

## Outcome and duration

Lodgement held; breakout achieved later in July (Cobra). Duration of the *campaign slice*
is **weeks**; a single Strategos scenario must pick **one day and one corps front** and
state that narrowing in the scenario blurb.
