## Terrain character

Rocky wooded spur (Little Round Top), steep western/southern slopes, limited fields of
fire through trees and boulders; open approach from the west/southwest once clear of
timber. High ground dominates the Union left.

## Objectives

- **Union:** Hold the left flank of the Army of the Potomac; deny Confederate occupation
  of the hill.
- **Confederate:** Seize Little Round Top to unhinge the Union left / enfilade Cemetery Ridge.

## Outcome and duration

Union holds after a bayonet charge when ammunition runs low; Confederate assault fails.
Fight lasts on the order of **1–2 hours** of intense action within the larger 2 July
battle (full day is army-scale — out of scope for this digest).
