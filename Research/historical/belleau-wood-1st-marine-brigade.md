## Terrain character

Dense woodland (Belleau Wood) bordered by open wheat fields; limited observation in the
timber; villages (Lucy-le-Bocage, Bouresches) as anchors. Machine-gun fields of fire from
wood edge into approaches.

## Objectives

- **U.S.:** Clear Belleau Wood; secure Bouresches; deny German advance on the Paris road
  axis in this sector.
- **German:** Hold the wood as a strongpoint / delay Allied advance.

## Outcome and duration

Wood cleared after roughly **three weeks** of intermittent heavy fighting; high U.S.
casualties; German withdrawal from the wood. A playable scenario should cover **one
attack day** (e.g. 6 June) at battalion scale, not the entire three weeks.
