## Forces (approximate)

**SOURCED:**

- **U.S. — elements of CCB, 9th Armored Division:** armor and mechanized infantry that
  reached Remagen and found the Ludendorff Bridge intact.
- **German — bridge security / demolition detail** under higher corps command: small
  garrison, not a full division defense. Exact company serials at the span are
  **UNKNOWN — do not invent** beyond a generic bridge guard.

## Terrain character

Rhine crossing at Remagen; steep approaches; town and rail bridge as the decisive point.
Playable map is a **procedural approximation** with culture bridges — not the real gorge DEM.

## Objectives

- **U.S.:** Seize and hold the Ludendorff Bridge before demolition succeeds; establish a
  foothold on the far bank.
- **German:** Hold or deny the crossing (demolition / delay).

## Outcome and duration

Bridge seized intact on 7 March; subsequent German attempts failed to destroy it before
US buildup. A playable scenario covers the **coup hours**, not the multi-day expansion.
