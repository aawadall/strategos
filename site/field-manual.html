What this is

Short command vocabulary for PLAY — not the DRILLS binder, not context help on one control, and not a full doctrine wiki yet. In the Windows alpha: Esc → FIELD MANUAL. Terms that cite drills (for example T1) cross-link with the DRILLS tab.

Source of truth: alpha-glossary.json · design note: docs/field-manual.md.

Terms

MoveTo

Order a unit to march to a map cell. Paths use terrain-cost A*. Arm MOVE (M) or right-click empty ground. Hold Shift to append behind the live plan instead of replacing it.

Related drills: T1

Engage

Direct-fire order against a hostile contact. Arm ENGAGE (E) or right-click a red contact. Range and line of sight still matter; this is not artillery.

Hold / Dig in

Stay put and prepare. Dig-in takes about two minutes; once prepared, the unit takes half the incoming fire. DIG IN on the palette bridges to the same Hold/Defend path when the unit can dig in.

Abort plan

Clear the selected unit's queued orders. Issued as a logged Abort command so replay can reconstruct the cut. Distinct from Hold — Abort means stop and do nothing.

ORBAT

Order of battle: the side's unit tree. Formations own subordinates; an order to a formation decomposes one echelon per step. Select from the map or the ORBAT list.

TTP / drill

Tactics, techniques, and procedures — coded drills in the DRILLS binder. Readiness gates which drills you may issue. Glossary terms may cite drill codes such as T1.

Related drills: T1
